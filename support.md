# FocusCal Support

Need help with FocusCal, or found something that isn't working? Email **[jwelshwhincup@gmail.com](mailto:jwelshwhincup@gmail.com)** and you'll get a reply within two working days.

## Common questions

**How do I choose which apps to block?**
Open Settings › Focus › Blocked Apps.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › FocusCal Pro.

**How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?**
Open Settings in FocusCal and tap Restore Purchases.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device and in your own iCloud. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
