FocusCal Privacy Policy
Last updated: 20 October 2026

FocusCal is a calendar that helps you protect your time. It's built so that your information stays with you. There's no FocusCal account, no advertising, and no tracking.

What stays on your device or in your iCloud
Your calendar. Events, tasks, favourite places and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other Apple devices. We can't see this data; only you can.
Your onboarding answers. The apps you said distract you, the times you want to protect, and your phone-time estimate stay on this device. They're only used to word your promise inside the app.
Screen Time. If you choose apps to block, Apple's Screen Time gives FocusCal an anonymous token for each one. FocusCal never learns which apps they are, and the tokens never leave your device.
Focus history. Your Focus sessions, and the reasons you give for ending a protected session early, stay on this device.
What is sent to Apple
Weather. To warn you about bad weather for an event, FocusCal sends the event's location (or your current location, if you allow it) to Apple's WeatherKit service.
Places and travel times. Searching for a place, or working out when to leave, sends your search or the locations involved to Apple Maps.
Purchases. Subscriptions are bought and managed through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details; FocusCal never sees them.
Apple's use of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What is sent to RevenueCat
FocusCal uses RevenueCat to check whether you have FocusCal Pro. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for this app, your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, and basic device information such as your iOS version and country. This information isn't linked to your name or email address, and isn't used for advertising.

Location
FocusCal only asks for your location while you're using the app, and only uses it for weather and travel times. You can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Notifications
Reminders, the morning briefing and the free-week reminder are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

What we don't do
We don't sell or share your data.
We don't use analytics or advertising tools.
We don't track you across other apps or websites.
Children
FocusCal isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data
Deleting FocusCal removes everything stored on your device. To remove synced data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › FocusCal and delete it. To stop your subscription, go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
